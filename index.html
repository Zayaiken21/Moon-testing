How to play

Nothing in Voxelia can be hurt. Animals run, hide, or come closer — that is the whole range of what happens between you and them.

W A S DWalk. Hold Shift to sprint. SpaceJump. Tap again mid-air once a Bramblehop follows you. MouseMove to look. Left button mines, right button places, middle button copies a block. If the page blocks pointer capture, hold a button and drag to look. 1 – 9Choose a hotbar slot. The scroll wheel works too. EFeed, tame, collect from, or dismiss the nearest animal. FToggle flight in creative mode. IOpen the block inspector and the block maker. EscPause. GamepadLeft stick moves, right stick looks, A jumps, X interacts, triggers mine and place, d-pad changes slot. TouchLeft half of the screen is a movement stick, right half looks. Buttons sit along the right edge.

Taming, step by step

Break the flora in a biome until you gather its food — Sunberries from Vale canopy, Tidefruit from coral, Lumenspores from glowing caps. Walk up to that biome's animal with the food in your satchel and press feed. It follows you from then on, lends you its ability, and leaves something to collect every so often.
